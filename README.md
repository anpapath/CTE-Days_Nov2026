A hands-on session for curious students: discover how ChatGPT-style AIs think, build your own AI librarian, and send an AI detective to solve a mystery at Hogwarts!

## 🧭 How to use a notebook
- Press **Shift + Enter** to run a cell, going from top to bottom.
- Cells marked ✏️ are for you to change. Edit them and run them again!
- Labs 1 and 2 ask for an **API key** at the start. Your teacher will give it to you during the session.
- Something broken? **Runtime → Restart and run all**.

## 📖 What's inside
- `notebooks/`: the three Colab notebooks
- `knowledge_base/`: the documents our AI librarian reads, one folder per theme (Harry Potter, Minecraft, Pokémon, football), plus a made-up café and planet that no AI could know about!

## 🛡️ Using AI responsibly
- AI can sound confident and still be wrong, so double-check important facts.
- Never share personal information (address, passwords, photos) with a chatbot.
- Use AI to help you learn, not to skip learning!
